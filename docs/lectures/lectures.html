Lectures

Class-meeting slides for Units 4–7.

Slides are organized by unit and class meeting. For example, 4.1 is the first meeting of Unit 4.

See the schedule for meeting dates and preparation. Readings and handouts are available on Canvas.

Lecture Focus
Lecture 4.1: Conceptual Framework and Theory Build a model, derive hypotheses, and discuss proof and mathiness.
Lecture 4.2: Reporting the Conceptual Framework Write theory clearly and connect hypotheses to identification.
Lecture 5.1: Data Collection: Surveys Connect sampling, questionnaire design, and measurement to the research question.
Lecture 5.2: Data Collection via API Plan reproducible retrieval and weigh data quantity against quality.
Lecture 5.3: Managing Data for Reproducibility: Git I Automate a project and record clear, recoverable changes.
Lecture 5.4: Managing Data for Reproducibility: Git II Use keys, reusable code, documentation, and clear collaboration practices.
Lecture 5.5: Data: Reporting Write a transparent data section with sample construction and measurement limits.
Lecture 6.1: Econometric Models and Identification Distinguish structural and reduced-form questions, selection, and the experimental ideal.
Lecture 6.2: Causality and Policy Evaluation Assess RD, difference-in-differences, synthetic controls, and credibility checks.
Lecture 6.3: Machine Learning in Economic Research Distinguish prediction from causal inference and evaluate model selection.
Lecture 6.4: Experiments, Qualitative and Mixed Methods Compare controlled variation with open-ended evidence and design a mixed-methods study.
Lecture 6.5: Evaluating Empirical Evidence Check the estimand, measurement, uncertainty, and economic significance.
Lecture 7.1: Reviewing Research Evaluate contribution, credibility, and clarity; write actionable referee comments.
Lecture 7.2: Responding to Reviewers Plan revisions, answer criticism with evidence, and communicate disagreements clearly.
