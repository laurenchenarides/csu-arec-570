Fall 2026 Course Schedule and Readings

Preparation: Complete each reading before the class period in which it is listed. All readings and assigned book excerpts are available on Canvas. See the course reading list for full references.

Lecture numbers identify the unit and meeting within that unit: Lecture 4.1 is the first meeting of Unit 4, and Lecture 4.2 is the second. The course introduction, recess, and final presentations are unnumbered.
